# Discord Poller

This project coordinates attendance for recurring tabletop roleplaying games in Discord.

## Language

**Game group**:
A recurring roleplaying activity with its own player roster, schedule, and private Discord channel. A person may belong to more than one game group.
_Avoid_: Campaign

**Group organizer**:
A game group member who manages that group's roster and schedule, and may appoint additional organizers.

**Group creator**:
A person with the server's group-creation permission who creates a game group and becomes its first organizer.

**Game session**:
One occurrence of a game group's recurring activity.
_Avoid_: Event

**Campaign**:
The fictional story being played by a game group. It is a label, not a separate attendance group, unless independent tracking is needed.

**Attendance response**:
A game group member's answer for a game session: attending or not attending. No response is a distinct state.
